**Starlite for iPhone 0.2.0**

- **Search:** the magnifying glass at the right of the tab bar searches everything at once: your playlists, liked and downloaded songs, and Navidrome / Jellyfin.
- **Music Server:** Recently Played at the top. Your server's playlists also show up in the Playlists tab by themselves.
- **Playlists:** switch between the CD shelf and a plain list with the button at the top. Opening a playlist is smoother: the disc glides out and the page grows out of the cover.
- **The full player** is cleaner: plain controls, a speaker on each side of the volume, and lyrics (from lrclib.net, timed, tap a line to jump there). Tap the artist for more of their songs from your music. It follows your finger when you pull it down, and no colour is left behind.
- **Sound:** speed (keeping the pitch, or not), bass, clarity, balance, mono and a 10-band equalizer with presets.
- **Last.fm:** shows what you play and scrobbles it (your own API key, like on the computer).
- **Looks:** Solid panels by default; covers without a picture use the theme's colours; plain icons in Settings with the real Navidrome, Jellyfin and Last.fm logos; the app icon is black.

Discord status isn't possible on iPhone: Discord's Rich Presence talks to the Discord app on the same computer, which iOS doesn't allow.

**Install:** needs iOS 26. The IPA isn't signed: install it with AltStore or Sideloadly (they sign it with your Apple ID).
