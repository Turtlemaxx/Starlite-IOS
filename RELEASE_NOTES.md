**Starlite for iPhone 0.3.1**

- **The widget works when sideloaded** (Feather and others that can't set up an App Group): it now gets what's playing, the cover and its settings straight from the app on the phone itself (127.0.0.1, nothing outside the phone can reach it), and its buttons reach the player the same way. When Starlite isn't running, the widget shows the last song, paused.
- **Opening a playlist:** the CD slides out of its case, pops out and grows big in the middle of the screen with a spin, and the playlist opens out of the disc. Going back, it shrinks into its CD again. Settings → Layout → Animations still sets the speed or turns movement off.

**Install:** needs iOS 26. The IPA isn't signed: install it with Feather, AltStore or Sideloadly.
