**Starlite for iPhone 0.3.2**

- **The widget keeps up by itself:** it gets the next songs ahead of time (with their covers and colours) and moves on to the next one when a song ends, even while Starlite is in the background. After a button press it waits for the new song's cover before redrawing.
- The cover and the cover colour now show in the widget.
- The time left sits at the right edge of the widget.

**Install:** needs iOS 26. The IPA isn't signed: install it with Feather, AltStore or Sideloadly.
