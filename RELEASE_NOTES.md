**Starlite for iPhone 0.3.0**

- **A home-screen widget** (2×4): what's playing with its cover, a progress bar that runs by itself, and buttons for play / pause, previous, next and like. Tap it to open the player. Settings → Widget changes everything about it, with a live preview: five layouts, the background (blurred cover, theme, a colour, a gradient), colours, which pieces show (cover, song, artist, album, up next, progress, times, controls, logo), text size, weight and font, cover size and corners, control style and size, spacing.
- **Lyrics like Apple Music:** big lines that light up word by word as they're sung, following the exact playback time; the other lines dimmed and softly blurred, pauses as three breathing dots. A timing setting moves them earlier or later (for Bluetooth headphones).
- **Opening a playlist:** its CD pulls out, slides down, and the playlist grows out of the cover. Settings → Layout → Animations sets the speed or turns movement off.
- The "No playlists yet" card has an × and stays away once closed.
- Settings saved by an older version keep loading after an update.

**Install:** needs iOS 26. The IPA isn't signed: install it with AltStore or Sideloadly (they sign it with your Apple ID; the widget shares what's playing with the app through an App Group, which they set up too).
